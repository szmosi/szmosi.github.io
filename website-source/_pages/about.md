# Hi, I'm Marton.

*Data Science · Automation · Process Improvement*

I enjoy solving problems that sit somewhere between **data, technology, and the way people work**.

My approach is simple: **if it's repetitive, automate it; if it's messy, structure it; if it's complicated, understand it.**

I'm a data and technology-oriented professional based in Denmark, with a background in business administration and data science. I'm particularly interested in finding inefficiencies, understanding why systems behave the way they do, and developing practical solutions that make processes more reliable.

Whether that means working with large datasets, building Python scripts, exploring machine learning models, or connecting systems through APIs, I'm happiest when I can turn a complicated problem into something understandable and useful.

## What I Work With

**Data & Analytics**

Exploring, cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing data to uncover patterns and communicate useful insights.

**Programming & Automation**

Building Python-based solutions, working with databases and APIs, and simplifying repetitive processes.

**Machine Learning & AI**

Exploring NLP, computer vision, model evaluation, and the trade-offs between computational performance and practical usefulness.

**Business Processes**

Understanding how technical solutions fit into real workflows, where problems originate, and how processes can be improved sustainably.

## Selected Projects

My portfolio is a collection of experiments, coursework, and practical technical explorations.

- **Python Speed Lab** — Investigating the performance differences between Python, NumPy, Numba, Cython, and GPU acceleration.
- **NLP Election Analytics** — Comparing classical NLP and transformer-based approaches for sentiment analysis and topic modelling.
- **Road Damage Classification** — Exploring SVM and convolutional neural network approaches to image-based road damage detection.

You can find the methodology, results, and source code on the [Projects](/projects/) page.

## Beyond the Screen

When I'm not exploring a dataset or improving a workflow, I'm usually experimenting in the kitchen, playing strategic board games, or staying active through sports.

I believe curiosity is useful far beyond technology. Learning something new, questioning an established process, or trying a different approach often leads to the most interesting discoveries.

*Hope is the last thing to die... but a well-built script is a close second.*
